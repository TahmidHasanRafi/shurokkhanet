Section 5.1 · Equation 2

Why hashing a phone number does not protect it

Eleven digits behind seven fixed operator prefixes. The candidate space is about 7×108, so a published SHA-256 of a mobile number is the same as publishing the number. The keyed commitment in Equation 3 is the same identifier under a key the ledger never sees.
